# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: `search_media`, `get_item` and `purchase_download` tools in a dependency-free local MCP server.
- `stock-media-search` and `stock-media-licensing` skills, and the `find` and `buy` commands.
- Search fills in prices that the search results leave out, and attaches thumbnails so Claude can check the results.
- Purchases require a confirmed maximum price and never charge more than it.
